TikTok College Admissions

CS 315 Data Science for the Web · 2025

This project analyzes TikTok content created by college admissions consulting influencers to examine patterns of demographic representation and audience targeting. Using a combination of zero-shot classification, topic modeling, and rhetorical analysis, we find that influencer content disproportionately targets white and male students, while advice directed toward women and lower-income students relies more heavily on emotional and motivational appeals. These findings highlight how social media–based admissions guidance may reinforce existing inequalities in access to educational narratives
