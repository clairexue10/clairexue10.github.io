About Me

01 / BACKGROUND

Hi — I'm Yuhe (Claire) Xue, a Computer Science student at Wellesley College. My academic journey focuses on the intersection of systems, programming languages, and data science with a particular emphasis on understanding how technology impacts society.

At Wellesley, I've had the opportunity to work on diverse projects ranging from analyzing social media influence patterns to building data pipelines and exploring the ethics of technology in our curriculum and community.

02 / INTERESTS

My primary interests lie in three interconnected areas:

  • Systems & Programming Languages: I'm fascinated by how we design and build computing systems, and the languages we use to express computational ideas. I believe that well-designed systems and expressive languages are fundamental to creating reliable, efficient, and maintainable software.
  • Data Science & Society: I'm particularly interested in how data science intersects with social issues. My work on TikTok college admissions consultants and ethics of technology reflects my commitment to understanding how algorithms and data systems shape social outcomes and perpetuate or challenge existing inequalities.
  • Research & Analysis: I enjoy building pipelines for data collection and analysis, combining both qualitative and quantitative methods to understand complex phenomena. I believe in rigorous, ethical research that considers both technical excellence and social responsibility.

03 / EXPERIENCE

Through my coursework and projects at Wellesley College, I've developed skills in:

  • Building data collection and analysis pipelines
  • Conducting qualitative and quantitative research
  • Analyzing social media data and algorithmic systems
  • Studying systems design and implementation
  • Exploring the ethical implications of technology

I'm always eager to learn more and take on new challenges that combine technical depth with social awareness.
